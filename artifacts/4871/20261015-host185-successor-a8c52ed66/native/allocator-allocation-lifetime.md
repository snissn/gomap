Geometry limits remain explicit: capture copies each changed/replaced Go map under the original allocator lock and reserves `1+len(map)` records. This is not incremental across a mutable map; at >31 entries Q32 may refuse indefinitely. `256+128*n` is a conservative **work-byte** bound, not exact Go-map allocation capacity or a PRIMARY lifetime charge. Ledger rollback likewise admits the whole actual candidate-ID vector and burned-tail replacement shape. These paths require either bounded canonical metadata storage/traversal or explicit pre-activation geometry refusal; current small allocator tests do not qualify arbitrary history/input32 geometry.

No generic generation refcount framework, transitive enrollment, charge-until-index-close policy, private arena or independently retained handle pool is authorized by this inventory. Existing canonical DATA roots remain authoritative. New controls/scratch must be charged and detached truthfully at their actual original owner.
