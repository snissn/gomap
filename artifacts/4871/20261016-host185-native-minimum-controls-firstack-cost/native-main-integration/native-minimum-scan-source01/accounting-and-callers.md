The production change is confined to nativeMVCCSnapshotMinimumV6 and its local reserve/price helpers in db/native_mvcc_allocator_preparation_v6.go. No resource/reference/owner/pager/registry pointer was added. The observer remains inline in nativeMVCCAllocatorPreparationV6, itself inline in NativeMVCCCurrentDeleteV6 and the original admitted cursor frame. There is no new backing allocation, lifetime edge, retention domain or refund. No compiler/runtime size observation was executed; every actual admission uses unsafe.Sizeof, including the changed containing types.

Let S=sizeof(nativeMVCCSnapshotMinimumV6), A=sizeof(snapshotAcquireRO[0]), I=sizeof(scanIndex), E=(1,S+32), V=(1,2*sizeof(epoch)+2*S+32), L=(1,2*A+2*I+S+32), M(n)=(1+n,32*n+2*S+32), and R(f)=the original lifecycle.NativeMinimumPriceForTestV6(f)=(17+f,288+8*f), conservatively including dirty fallback. All sums below are vector sums (records,bytes).

* Every call first admits E, then V before its epoch load, comparison, initialization/reset or completed-result reuse. Failure to admit either does not inspect/update observer evidence.
* Initial and terminal calls admit L before EACH actual atomic counter load and its saved index/reset. A positive shard discards the whole attempt. Zero advances scanIndex persistently. Up to the remaining allowance may be consumed in one call; each row leaves persistent progress.
* Once all 256 loads of a pass are saved, V is admitted before the final epoch load/phase transition. A refused transition remains at index256 and retries E+V+V, without reloading that pass. Changed epoch discards the complete attempt.
* Registry calls retain original idxMu scope and admit M(n) before selecting the next original generation/current fallback. R(f) is admitted by the original registry before scanning/caching. Index/currentSeen and registry-to-terminal transition persist exactly as before. A registry refusal may replay E+V+M next entry; the next-unit expression includes it.
* A completed observer admits E+V again before returning its scalar minimum. An epoch mismatch restarts it. No unvalidated minimum is cached through a containing refusal.

NativeSnapshotMinimumPriceForTestV6(n,f) is the conservative NEXT persistent-progress/result superunit E+V+L+V+M(n)+R(f), using checked sums. Safety relies on unchanged producers: real births publish positive shard count before begin epoch/view; completion publishes end epoch before dropping count. With SAME epoch sampled before initial load and checked through final scan, an old unregistered acquisition is either observed positive or changes epoch. A complete begin/end between yields, including on an already-scanned shard, changes epoch and invalidates evidence. Existing registered old readers are covered by original coherent registry minima and first-cohort exclusion. No lock is held across yields. The later authority/capability race has the same original rules; this packet does not supply new protection there.

Limits deliberately remain explicit:

* Whole idxAll selection and dirty fallback are unchanged indivisible source units; their FULL E+V+M+R prefix must fit. Some geometries can still yield indefinitely when their component unit fits total limits but the prefix does not. * Continuous legal acquisition/epoch churn may restart forever. The protected writer-after-every-yield fixture is unchanged. Only safety under churn and convergence after it stops are tested; there is no unconditional finite total or wait-free guarantee.
* Epoch wraparound assumptions and generation-monotonicity are inherited. No new global evidence, active-reader counter, owner, or registration protocol was introduced.
* No public ACK/publication/Finish assertion was executed on this source. The shared actual oracle/meter, physical41/9 bounds and six-arm fixture are byte-identical; future runs derive fresh G through the updated helper.
