2. **Safety if restart is simply removed:** after the observer has passed a counter position and observed a generation registry, a birth loads an older PRIMARY-backed view but has not registered. ## Why existing minima do not supply the missing invariant

ReaderRegistry's fixed 16 cohorts are useful existing authority. Under r.mu, MinPinnedSeq/WithWork observe all fast cohorts coherently; native cohort claims also hold that mutex. Joining an already-positive cohort does not introduce a lower sequence. But `r.min` caches only slow `seqs`, excludes fast shards, and becomes dirty when a slow minimum unregisters. A direct scalar load of r.min is not the combined minimum. WithWork currently admits 17 fixed records plus the whole dirty fallback vector; arbitrary old ordinary readers can grow that vector. It neither solves the birth gap nor guarantees Q32 traversal for unbounded history. Retaining a lower-ever scalar is safe only as permanent over-retention; it does not prove memory/reuse scales with the declared quantum.
